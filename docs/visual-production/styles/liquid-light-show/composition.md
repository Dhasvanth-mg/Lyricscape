# Liquid Light Show: composition families

Style 8 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


5 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## LLS-CMP-C1 Full-bleed dish with band slide

- **Focal point:** The band slide.
- **Lyric placement:** Dark band across the middle or lower third.
- **Background structure:** Fluid full frame.
- **Foreground:** Band.
- **Negative space:** None (colour field).
- **Visual hierarchy:** Band text > fluid.
- **Layering (back to front):** fluid, band, text, lamp grain
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** None.
- **Safe areas:** Band text inside central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** FLU, SLD band
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Verses, dense lines.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Band across.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Band in the lower third.
  - 1080x1080 square: Band middle.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Band lower third.

## LLS-CMP-C2 Letter-mask slide

- **Focal point:** Big letters filled with moving colour.
- **Lyric placement:** Centred letters.
- **Background structure:** Black slide (the rest of the frame), with a soft glow halo of the fluid leaking at the slide edges (10%).
- **Foreground:** Letters.
- **Negative space:** Black.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Letters.
- **Layering (back to front):** fluid, black slide with letter holes, halo
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** None.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** FLU, SLD letter mask
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Hooks and short lines.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: 1-2 lines.
  - 720x1280 portrait: 2-3 lines stacked.
  - 1080x1080 square: 2 lines.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Short lines only.

## LLS-CMP-C3 Visible dish

- **Focal point:** A round projected dish on darkness.
- **Lyric placement:** Slide band across the dish.
- **Background structure:** Darkness around a circle of fluid.
- **Foreground:** Dish rim and meniscus.
- **Negative space:** Darkness (35%).
- **Visual hierarchy:** Slide > dish.
- **Layering (back to front):** darkness, dish fluid, rim, slide
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Slow drift of the dish.
- **Safe areas:** Slide inside the dish and central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** DSH, FLU
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Intros, quiet sections, breaks.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Dish 90% height.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Dish 90% width.
  - 1080x1080 square: Dish 90%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Dish fills width.

## LLS-CMP-C4 Double projection

- **Focal point:** The overlap of two dishes.
- **Lyric placement:** Slide in the overlap region.
- **Background structure:** Two dishes offset, additive.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Small.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Slide > overlap > dishes.
- **Layering (back to front):** dish A, dish B (additive), slide
- **Depth and perspective:** Two layers.
- **Camera:** Dishes drift apart and together.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** FLU x2, DSH pair
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Dishes side by side overlapping.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Stacked overlapping.
  - 1080x1080 square: Diagonal.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Stacked.

## LLS-CMP-C5 Colour wheel

- **Focal point:** A segmented wheel tinting the fluid.
- **Lyric placement:** Band slide.
- **Background structure:** Fluid tinted by the wheel.
- **Foreground:** Wheel segments visible at the frame edge as soft-edged sectors.
- **Negative space:** None.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Slide > wheel > fluid.
- **Layering (back to front):** fluid, wheel tint, wheel edge, slide
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** None.
- **Safe areas:** Central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** WHL, FLU
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Bridges, choruses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wheel edge at the right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Wheel edge at the bottom.
  - 1080x1080 square: Corner.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Bottom.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Accumulating dyes within a dish; dishes swap at sections. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
