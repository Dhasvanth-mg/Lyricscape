## Composition grammar

**Layer order (back to front):**

1. ground gradient (light rig)
2. far layer (tracing paper, lightest)
3. middle set
4. near set
5. figures and moving pieces
6. props (strings, bunting, falling paper)
7. lyric (card, banner or cut layer)
8. frame (arch, window, book, lantern, rollers)
9. post: paper fibre on ground, fog on far layers

**Required.** Ground light; at least 3 layers from one set (far, middle, near); one frame (except C6); one lyric treatment.

**Optional.** Figures (max 4 on stage), props (max 2 kinds), curtain.

**Scale and position constraints.** Far pieces 10-30% of height, middle 20-45%, near 30-60%; figures 8-25%; no piece over 40% except frames and C6.

**Typography-safe regions.** Lyric on the brightest ground region and inside the frame opening's central 80%; no piece may cover more than 8% of the lyric box (overlap test).

**Colour compatibility.** One ground hue per stage; one tissue accent; pieces black or set paper only.

**Motion compatibility.** Max 3 moving elements (intensity tier caps); no piece crosses the lyric box while a word is being revealed.

**Material compatibility.** Tissue only for light-transmitting pieces; tracing paper only on the far layer; kraft and black card never on the same layer.

**Complexity limits.** Max 20 pieces on stage; max 7 layers.

**Invalid combinations.**

| This | never with | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Two sets | same stage | Breaks the world (only panorama seams join sets). |
| Cut letters | long lines over 3 words | Becomes unreadable; use a printed card. |
| Lantern composition | more than 2 figures | Too small to read. |
| Fairground light cycle | quiet or sad identity | Wrong mood. |
| Snow village | religious symbols | Excluded everywhere. |
| Tissue accent | behind the lyric | Coloured light lowers contrast unpredictably. |

## Meaningful variation

**Major (change the scene):**
- Set (13 worlds)
- Composition (proscenium, window, pop-up, lantern, panorama, close-up, tiny stage)
- Time of day and light rig
- Cast (figures and their choreography)
- Paper type (black card, layered greys, kraft, tissue)

**Minor (parameters, not new scenes):**
- Ground hue
- Arrangement seed inside a set composition
- Prop choice

**Not counted as assets or as variety:** Mirrored pieces, hue changes, arrangement seeds.

## Deterministic selection

Set from identity subjects and lyric words (a lexicon per set, multilingual), ties by hash(trackId). Composition per section from role compatibility in a seeded order; chorus fixes its composition at first appearance. Light rig from mood and cover. Set compositions (arrangements) chosen by hash within the set.

## Repetition control

A set lasts 1-3 sections; at most 2 sets per song (3 for songs over 5 minutes); figures used at most twice per song; no composition twice in a row except chorus recall.
