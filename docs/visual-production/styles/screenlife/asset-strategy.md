## Why this scale

Phase 1 estimated 250-350 originals. Phase 2 approves 219 live entries. The 16 app kits are counted as one entry per kit plus their states and widgets as components; screen recolours and theme variants are not counted.

## Families

### SCR-UIK-APP Fictional app kits (16 sources)

- **Purpose:** Each kit is a kind of writing, which is the main variation axis of the style (Phase 1 E.1).
- **Design language:** Original design language: rounded-rect geometry on an 8 px grid, the style's neutral ramps, one accent; invented names; no real iconography.
- **Production:** authored-data; JSON layout spec (widgets, metrics, text styles) painted at runtime to a canvas sheet; icons as inline SVG path data (original).
- **Dimensions:** Layout in device points (phone 390x844 pt, tablet 820x1180, laptop 1440x900, terminal 80x24 cells); painted at 2x into sheets up to 2048 px.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** Dark and light themes and accent recolours are parameters, not separate assets.
- **Compatibility:** Device frames of the matching class; typing profiles; notifications of the same device world. **Exclusions:** Never two kits of different OS design languages in one song; terminal kits never with stickers.
- **Quality bar:** Reads as a believable original app at a glance; passes the brand-likeness review; lyric field contrast 4.5:1; all states painted in dark and light themes; RTL mirror correct.

### SCR-UIK-STA Kit states (53 components)

- **Purpose:** States are what change within an app across a section, so a verse can evolve without changing app.
- **Design language:** A specific screen state of a kit with a defined active field and slots for earlier lines.
- **Production:** authored-data; JSON state spec referencing its kit; painted to the kit sheet.
- **Dimensions:** As the kit.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Typing profiles; transitions send-scroll and app-switch. **Exclusions:** Upcoming lyric lines may appear only in states that declare a "suggestion" slot and only for timed lyrics.
- **Quality bar:** Active field rect inside the composition safe area in C1 and C2 framing; state is distinguishable from its siblings in a thumbnail.

### SCR-FRM-DEV Device frames (10 sources)

- **Purpose:** The device decides scale, framing and the world of the song (phone intimacy, laptop work, CRT memory).
- **Design language:** Invented, generic industrial design: no camera bump shapes, logos or button layouts that identify a real product.
- **Production:** handcrafted-vector; SVG source (exported to a 2x texture at build) with a screen-rect metadata file.
- **Dimensions:** Phone 1180x2400 px texture; laptop 2400x1600; CRT 2000x1700; car display 2400x1000.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Kits of the matching device class. **Exclusions:** Hand silhouette only with phones and tablets.
- **Quality bar:** No recognisable real product silhouette; screen rect aligned within 1 px at 2x; reads at 25% frame height.

### SCR-ENV-ROM Room plates (11 sources)

- **Purpose:** Places the device in a life (bedroom, kitchen, train) and gives depth and light colour.
- **Design language:** Out-of-focus environments: shapes, light and bokeh only, never detailed objects; painted as large soft forms with one practical light.
- **Production:** hybrid; Layered SVG of soft shapes rendered to a 1024x576 texture plus a bokeh layer generated by PRC-BOK.
- **Dimensions:** 16:9 master 2048x1152 painted at 1024x576 (it is always blurred); portrait crop rule per plate.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Phones, tablets, laptops; not CRT (CRT uses its own dark room). **Exclusions:** Never two practical lights of different temperatures in one plate.
- **Quality bar:** Reads as a place in deep blur; no object identifiable enough to raise rights questions; light direction matches the declared focal light.

### SCR-PRC-WAL Wallpaper generators (8 configurations)

- **Purpose:** Lock screen and desktop backgrounds that carry the song's colour.
- **Design language:** Wallpapers made from the cover or from pure geometry, never stock photos.
- **Production:** procedural-code; Generator config JSON for the shared cover-derivation generator (SHR-PRC-CVR).
- **Dimensions:** Rendered at the device screen size.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Halo and Plaid kits. **Exclusions:** Cover crop wallpapers are not used in the Post and caption family (the cover already appears unmodified there).
- **Quality bar:** Never alters the cover when shown as the cover; abstract ones contain no recognisable imagery.

### SCR-PRP-NTF Notifications and system overlays (14 components)

- **Purpose:** Fill gaps meaningfully and mark acts (delivered, read, missed) using only song metadata.
- **Design language:** Kit-consistent banners and badges.
- **Production:** authored-data; JSON widget spec painted to an overlay sheet.
- **Dimensions:** Device points.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Kits of the same device world. **Exclusions:** Max 1 on screen besides Halo stack; never over the lyric field.
- **Quality bar:** Text is only metadata or sung lines; does not obstruct the lyric box.

### SCR-PRP-STK Original reaction stickers (18 sources)

- **Purpose:** One playful punctuation per chorus in Messages and Post.
- **Design language:** Flat, thick-outlined original stickers in the accent and complementary colours; no Unicode emoji artwork.
- **Production:** handcrafted-vector; SVG source, rasterised to 256 px sprites.
- **Dimensions:** 256x256 with 8 px safe border.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Murmur, Frame, Tick kits. **Exclusions:** Never in terminal, code editor, old desktop; never on sad or grief identity unless chosen.
- **Quality bar:** Readable at 48 px; no resemblance to emoji sets.

### SCR-REV-CAR Caret, selection and correction overlays (10 components)

- **Purpose:** The visible act of writing and word-level emphasis.
- **Design language:** Kit-matched carets and highlights.
- **Production:** procedural-code; Code-drawn quads with parameters.
- **Dimensions:** Relative to font size.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Typing profiles, emphasis rules. **Exclusions:** Selection sweep and autocorrect never on estimated words; no squiggles in complex scripts.
- **Quality bar:** Caret position exact at grapheme boundaries in all scripts tested; blink stays within flash limits.

### SCR-MOT-TYP Typing behaviour profiles (10 configurations)

- **Purpose:** Who is writing changes the feeling of the scene without changing a cue.
- **Design language:** Rhythm models of a person writing.
- **Production:** authored-data; JSON: burst length distribution, pause distribution, typo rate, correction style, finish fraction.
- **Dimensions:** n/a
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Any kit; energy tiers. **Exclusions:** Typo-bearing profiles disabled for complex scripts and estimated timing (typos only in Latin, line or authored).
- **Quality bar:** Finishes every line before its end in the timing test; profiles distinguishable by a viewer in an A/B test of the same line.

### SCR-MOT-TPO Typo models (6 configurations)

- **Purpose:** Human imperfection that is always corrected before the cue.
- **Design language:** Rules for plausible mistakes in Latin text.
- **Production:** authored-data; JSON rule set plus a QWERTY-adjacency table (Latin only).
- **Dimensions:** n/a
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Profiles that declare typos. **Exclusions:** Never for non-Latin scripts, never in the last word of a line, never in a hook's first appearance.
- **Quality bar:** A typo is always corrected at least 150 ms before the word end.

### SCR-CAM-CAM Camera moves (12 configurations)

- **Purpose:** Direct attention and give the over-the-shoulder feel.
- **Design language:** Operator moves with physical inertia.
- **Production:** authored-data; JSON camera path: keyframes in normalised scene space, spring parameters.
- **Dimensions:** n/a
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** All kits. **Exclusions:** No camera move during the last 300 ms of a line's typing; no handheld in reduced motion.
- **Quality bar:** Lyric stays inside the safe area during the whole move at all four test sizes.

### SCR-LAY-FLD Lyric field treatments (10 components)

- **Purpose:** Guarantees the lyric is the largest and clearest text in every kit.
- **Design language:** How the lyric sits inside each kind of field.
- **Production:** authored-data; JSON: field padding, max lines, font role, scale-up rule.
- **Dimensions:** Device points.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** All kits declare which treatment they use. **Exclusions:** No treatment may place the lyric under 2.2% x-height in output pixels.
- **Quality bar:** Contrast and x-height floors met in all test sizes.

### SCR-PRC-META Metadata content rules (8 configurations)

- **Purpose:** Keeps every screen believable without inventing other people's words.
- **Design language:** Rules that fill non-lyric UI text from song metadata only.
- **Production:** procedural-code; JSON rule table: slot -> metadata source.
- **Dimensions:** n/a
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** All kits. **Exclusions:** No rule may produce text that is not the title, artist, album, a sung line, song time or a line number.
- **Quality bar:** Unit test: every rendered non-lyric string comes from an allowed source.

### SCR-TEX-GLS Glass and CRT surface plates (6 sources)

- **Purpose:** Material realism at very low strength; makes screens feel like objects.
- **Design language:** Subtle physical surfaces over screens.
- **Production:** photographed; Greyscale PNG or KTX2 (BC4/ETC2 R8) tileable textures.
- **Dimensions:** 1024x1024 tileable; CRT mask 512x512 tile.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** Device frames. **Exclusions:** Glass smudge never with CRT; nothing over the lyric field above 4% opacity.
- **Quality bar:** Invisible as "a texture" at normal viewing; no visible tiling seams.
