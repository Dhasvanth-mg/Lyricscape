# Ink: composition families

Style 11 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


5 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## INK-CMP-C1 Element and column

- **Focal point:** The painted element and the large word.
- **Lyric placement:** Element in one third, column in the opposite third.
- **Background structure:** Paper.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** At least 55% empty paper.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Large word > column > element.
- **Layering (back to front):** paper, wash, element, type
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Type inside central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** ELM families, PAP
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Default.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Element left, column right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Element top, column bottom.
  - 1080x1080 square: Diagonal.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Element smaller.

## INK-CMP-C2 Single word

- **Focal point:** One large brushed word.
- **Lyric placement:** Centred or on a third.
- **Background structure:** Paper.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Most of the sheet.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Word.
- **Layering (back to front):** paper, word
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Push.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** PAP, SRV
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Short lines, hooks.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Word 50% width.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Word 80% width.
  - 1080x1080 square: Word 70%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Word fills.

## INK-CMP-C3 Water register

- **Focal point:** Clean type over ink clouds.
- **Lyric placement:** Type centred; clouds behind.
- **Background structure:** Clear water (white or black).
- **Foreground:** Particles.
- **Negative space:** Clear water.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Type > clouds.
- **Layering (back to front):** water, bloom clouds, type
- **Depth and perspective:** Bloom depth.
- **Camera:** Slow drift.
- **Safe areas:** Type central 80% with a clean halo.
- **Compatible asset categories:** BLM water, WTR
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Bridges, ambient.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Clouds spread.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Clouds rise.
  - 1080x1080 square: Centre.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Type larger.

## INK-CMP-C4 Landscape scroll

- **Focal point:** A wide landscape painted across the frame with type in the sky.
- **Lyric placement:** Landscape low, type in the sky.
- **Background structure:** Paper.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Sky.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Type > landscape.
- **Layering (back to front):** paper, landscape, type
- **Depth and perspective:** Atmospheric wash depth (pale far).
- **Camera:** Slow pan along the scroll.
- **Safe areas:** Type in the upper central area.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LND elements
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses, open songs.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide scroll.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical hanging scroll.
  - 1080x1080 square: Crop.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Type larger.

## INK-CMP-C5 Fragment

- **Focal point:** A close crop of a stroke texture with the word beside it.
- **Lyric placement:** Stroke fills half.
- **Background structure:** Paper.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Paper half.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Word > stroke.
- **Layering (back to front):** paper, stroke, type
- **Depth and perspective:** Macro.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Type central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** BRS
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Intense hooks (heavy brush), rap.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Stroke left.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Stroke top.
  - 1080x1080 square: Corner.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Type larger.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

The sheet accumulates within a section (earlier lines fade to pale wash); sections take a new sheet. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
