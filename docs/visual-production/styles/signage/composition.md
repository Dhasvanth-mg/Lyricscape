# Signage: composition families

Style 10 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


6 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## SIG-CMP-C1 Hero sign in a place

- **Focal point:** The sign's panel.
- **Lyric placement:** Sign at the upper-middle third; reflection below.
- **Background structure:** Setting plate.
- **Foreground:** Rain, wires.
- **Negative space:** Dark sky and wall.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Panel > housing > reflection > place.
- **Layering (back to front):** plate, sign, reflection, weather
- **Depth and perspective:** Street-level depth.
- **Camera:** Locked or slow dolly.
- **Safe areas:** Panel inside central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** SGN, SET, HWR
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Default.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Sign 40-60% width.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical sign or close crop.
  - 1080x1080 square: Sign 60%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Panel fills width.

## SIG-CMP-C2 The board

- **Focal point:** A wide board filling the frame.
- **Lyric placement:** Board straight on, slight perspective.
- **Background structure:** Hall darkness.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Unlit rows.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Active row > other rows.
- **Layering (back to front):** board, glass
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Slow push.
- **Safe areas:** Rows central 90%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** HWR split-flap and dot
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Word-synced verses, long lines.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Board 85% width.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Board tilted to a tall column of rows.
  - 1080x1080 square: Board 80%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Fewer cells per row.

## SIG-CMP-C3 Window sign

- **Focal point:** A neon word in a window.
- **Lyric placement:** Sign behind glass, off-centre.
- **Background structure:** Dim interior.
- **Foreground:** Glass, rain drops, reflections of the street.
- **Negative space:** Dark window.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Neon > glass > interior.
- **Layering (back to front):** interior, neon, glass, rain
- **Depth and perspective:** Through glass.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Neon central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** SGN window signs, NEO alphabets
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Quiet songs, short lines.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Window frame visible.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Tall window.
  - 1080x1080 square: Window crop.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Neon fills.

## SIG-CMP-C4 Marquee front

- **Focal point:** The marquee's changeable letters.
- **Lyric placement:** Marquee across the upper half, bulbs framing.
- **Background structure:** Theatre front.
- **Foreground:** Bulb glow.
- **Negative space:** Below marquee dark entrance.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Letters > bulbs > building.
- **Layering (back to front):** building, marquee, bulbs, bloom
- **Depth and perspective:** Low angle.
- **Camera:** Low angle locked.
- **Safe areas:** Letters central 88%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** SGN marquee, BLB
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses, showy pop.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide marquee.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical blade sign above a short marquee.
  - 1080x1080 square: Marquee 80%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Marquee fills.

## SIG-CMP-C5 Ticker on a structure

- **Focal point:** Scrolling LED ticker.
- **Lyric placement:** Ticker band across the frame.
- **Background structure:** Overpass, building or stadium.
- **Foreground:** Traffic light trails.
- **Negative space:** Sky.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Ticker > structure.
- **Layering (back to front):** plate, ticker, light trails
- **Depth and perspective:** Distance.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Ticker band at 40-60% height.
- **Compatible asset categories:** HWR ticker, SET
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Dense or fast lines.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Band full width.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Band crops; scroll slower.
  - 1080x1080 square: Band full.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Larger cells.

## SIG-CMP-C6 Wall of signs

- **Focal point:** The newest lit sign among many.
- **Lyric placement:** Signs stacked on a street wall (invented).
- **Background structure:** Street.
- **Foreground:** Wires.
- **Negative space:** Unlit signs.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Newest lit > others dim.
- **Layering (back to front):** plate, signs, wires, weather
- **Depth and perspective:** Layered façades.
- **Camera:** Pans to the lit sign.
- **Safe areas:** Lit sign inside central 80% after framing.
- **Compatible asset categories:** SGN many, SET dense street
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Chorus repeats, busy songs.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide street.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical stack of blade signs.
  - 1080x1080 square: Cluster.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Close framing.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Night deepens across the song (dusk to late night to pre-dawn). Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
