# Monument: composition families

Style 15 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


4 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## MON-CMP-C1 Façade ahead

- **Focal point:** The active façade facing the camera.
- **Lyric placement:** Centre, at eye height.
- **Background structure:** Previous façades in fog.
- **Foreground:** Ground.
- **Negative space:** Sky and fog.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Active façade > city.
- **Layering (back to front):** sky, fog, city, active façade, light, dust
- **Depth and perspective:** Deep.
- **Camera:** Walk toward.
- **Safe areas:** Façade text central 84% at reading moment.
- **Compatible asset categories:** EXT, ARC, MAT
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Default.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Façade 60% width.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Façade stacked storeys.
  - 1080x1080 square: 70%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Closer.

## MON-CMP-C2 Wall walk

- **Focal point:** A long wall of words beside the camera.
- **Lyric placement:** Wall on one side receding.
- **Background structure:** Fog.
- **Foreground:** Ground.
- **Negative space:** Opposite side.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Current word on the wall > rest.
- **Layering (back to front):** sky, wall, ground
- **Depth and perspective:** Deep.
- **Camera:** Walk beside.
- **Safe areas:** Current word inside central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** ARC canyon
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Long or dense lines.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wall left.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Wall more frontal.
  - 1080x1080 square: Wall left.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Closer.

## MON-CMP-C3 Look up

- **Focal point:** A monumental hook façade.
- **Lyric placement:** Low camera looking up.
- **Background structure:** Sky.
- **Foreground:** Base of the façade.
- **Negative space:** Sky.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Hook.
- **Layering (back to front):** sky, façade, light
- **Depth and perspective:** Low angle.
- **Camera:** Stop and tilt.
- **Safe areas:** Hook central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** EXT, LGT
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Façade fills height.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Natural.
  - 1080x1080 square: Fills.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Fills.

## MON-CMP-C4 Aerial

- **Focal point:** The city.
- **Lyric placement:** Top-down or high oblique.
- **Background structure:** Ground plane.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Edges.
- **Visual hierarchy:** City.
- **Layering (back to front):** ground, city, fog
- **Depth and perspective:** High.
- **Camera:** Pull back.
- **Safe areas:** Title text central if shown.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CTY
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Outro, breaks.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Tall.
  - 1080x1080 square: Centre.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Centre.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

The city grows line by line. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
