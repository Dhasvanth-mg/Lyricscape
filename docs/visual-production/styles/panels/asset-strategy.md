## Why this scale

Phase 1 estimated about 700 originals. Phase 2 approves 286 live entries. 162 are panel illustrations, the expensive family; each is drawn once as line, fill and shadow layers so the five art registers are treatments rather than five drawings (Phase 1 counted registers separately). More illustrations can be added in later batches without changing the system.

## Families

### PAN-ENV-PLC Place panels (30 sources)

- **Purpose:** Establishing shots: where the song is.
- **Design language:** Panel illustration drawn as clean line art plus flat fill and shadow layers, so every art register (halftone pop, screentone, ink noir, flat colour, newsprint) is a treatment of the same drawing; framed for a panel, with a declared balloon zone.
- **Production:** handcrafted-vector; SVG line art plus fill and shadow region masks (KTX2 R8 x3), balloon-zone rect, focal point; drawn at 2048 on the long side; three crops (wide, square, tall) declared as rects.
- **Dimensions:** 2048 on the long side; crops for wide, square and tall panels.
- **Variation dimensions inside the family:** member identity only (each member is a different design)
- **Compatibility:** All registers; balloon zone must fit a balloon of 8 words at panel size. **Exclusions:** No recurring characters; figures only as anonymous silhouettes, hands, backs or crowds; no identifiable people, brands or real landmarks.
- **Quality bar:** Reads at panel size on a phone; balloon zone clear; works in all registers (checked in the register review); not a copy of any existing comic panel.
