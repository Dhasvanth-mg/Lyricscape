# Cover Pop: composition families

Style 14 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


5 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## CVP-CMP-C1 Full cover phrase

- **Focal point:** A phrase on the full cover.
- **Lyric placement:** Phrase in the emptiest region (saliency).
- **Background structure:** Full cover.
- **Foreground:** Stickers.
- **Negative space:** Cover's own empty areas.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Chip > phrase > cover.
- **Layering (back to front):** cover, burst, phrase, chip, stickers
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Slow push.
- **Safe areas:** Phrase central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** Cover, PHL, CHP
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses, intros.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Cover fit to height with colour-extended sides (existing mono background), or cover fills width when it allows.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Cover fit to width, colour field above and below with phrases in the field.
  - 1080x1080 square: Cover fills.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Larger phrase.

## CVP-CMP-C2 Crop detail

- **Focal point:** A detail of the cover with a phrase.
- **Lyric placement:** Crop region from saliency; phrase in its empty part.
- **Background structure:** Cover crop.
- **Foreground:** Stickers.
- **Negative space:** Crop's empty area.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Phrase > crop.
- **Layering (back to front):** cover crop, phrase
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Pan path.
- **Safe areas:** Central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CRP, SAL
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Verses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide crop.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Tall crop.
  - 1080x1080 square: Square crop.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Tight.

## CVP-CMP-C3 Giant word

- **Focal point:** One word filling the frame over the cover.
- **Lyric placement:** Word centred, 60-90% width.
- **Background structure:** Cover.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Cover.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Word.
- **Layering (back to front):** cover, word
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Central 90%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** VOI
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Short lines, hooks.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Word 60% width.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Word 90% width.
  - 1080x1080 square: 80%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Fills.

## CVP-CMP-C4 Colour field and cover

- **Focal point:** Phrase in a colour field beside the cover.
- **Lyric placement:** Cover on one side, field on the other.
- **Background structure:** Field from the cover's dominant colour.
- **Foreground:** Stickers.
- **Negative space:** Field.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Phrase > cover.
- **Layering (back to front):** field, cover, phrase
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Phrase in the field.
- **Compatible asset categories:** COL, PHL
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Busy covers with no empty region; long phrases.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Cover left, field right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Cover top, field bottom.
  - 1080x1080 square: Cover inset.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Cover top.

## CVP-CMP-C5 Sticker collage

- **Focal point:** Phrase with stickers and cover cut-outs.
- **Lyric placement:** Phrase centre, cut-out stickers around.
- **Background structure:** Cover.
- **Foreground:** Cut-out stickers.
- **Negative space:** Cover.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Phrase > stickers.
- **Layering (back to front):** cover, cut-outs, phrase, stickers
- **Depth and perspective:** Shadows.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CUT, STK
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Playful songs, bridges.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Around.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Above and below.
  - 1080x1080 square: Around.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Fewer stickers.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Crop path explores the whole cover across the song. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
