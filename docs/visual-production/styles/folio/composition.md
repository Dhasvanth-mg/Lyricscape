# Folio: composition families

Style 6 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


8 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## FOL-CMP-C1 Pull-quote page

- **Focal point:** The pull quote in the page's quote slot.
- **Lyric placement:** Quote slot (template-defined: between columns, top of column, or margin).
- **Background structure:** Body columns of earlier lines at dim ink.
- **Foreground:** Rules framing the quote.
- **Negative space:** Margins.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Quote > rules > body > folio.
- **Layering (back to front):** desk, page paper, body, plates, rules, quote, marginalia
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat page, slight camera angle 2 degrees.
- **Camera:** Frame the quote slot.
- **Safe areas:** Quote inside central 80% of the frame after framing.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY quarterly and newspaper templates, PAIR, RUL
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Verses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Spread view, camera frames quote and its column.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Single page view.
  - 1080x1080 square: Single page view.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Close framing on the quote slot.

## FOL-CMP-C2 Headline spread

- **Focal point:** The hook as a headline.
- **Lyric placement:** Across the spread top.
- **Background structure:** Spread with plate.
- **Foreground:** Masthead or section head.
- **Negative space:** Generous.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Headline > plate > deck line.
- **Layering (back to front):** page, plate, headline, deck, body
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Pull back to the spread.
- **Safe areas:** Headline inside central 90%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY headline templates, PLT
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Spread.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Headline stacked on one page.
  - 1080x1080 square: Page.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Page, headline 3 lines max.

## FOL-CMP-C3 Drop-cap opening

- **Focal point:** A large drop cap and opening paragraph.
- **Lyric placement:** Paragraph at the start of an article.
- **Background structure:** Page.
- **Foreground:** Drop cap in spot colour.
- **Negative space:** Margins.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Drop cap > paragraph.
- **Layering (back to front):** page, drop cap, paragraph
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Frame the paragraph.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY opening templates, PAIR
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Openings of verses, long lines; Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew only.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Spread, paragraph on the right page.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Page.
  - 1080x1080 square: Page.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Close.

## FOL-CMP-C4 Plate and caption

- **Focal point:** A full plate with the lyric as its caption.
- **Lyric placement:** Caption below or beside the plate.
- **Background structure:** Page.
- **Foreground:** Plate in a frame.
- **Negative space:** Page margins around the plate.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Caption (lyric) > plate.
- **Layering (back to front):** page, plate, frame, caption
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Slow drift across the plate.
- **Safe areas:** Caption central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** PLT, FRM plate frames
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Breaks, quiet lines, art book.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Plate left, caption right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Plate top, caption below.
  - 1080x1080 square: Plate top.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Plate top.

## FOL-CMP-C5 Parallel text

- **Focal point:** Two columns: original and second line.
- **Lyric placement:** Original on the reading-start side, second line on the other.
- **Background structure:** Page.
- **Foreground:** Centre rule.
- **Negative space:** Margins.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Original active line > second line > earlier lines.
- **Layering (back to front):** page, columns, rule, line numbers
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Frame both columns.
- **Safe areas:** Both columns inside central 90%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY bilingual templates
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Second line enabled (translation or romanisation).
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Side by side.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Stacked original then second line.
  - 1080x1080 square: Side by side narrow.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Stacked.

## FOL-CMP-C6 Contents and index

- **Focal point:** The contents list with the current section highlighted.
- **Lyric placement:** Lines as entries with song times as page numbers.
- **Background structure:** Page.
- **Foreground:** Leader dots and rules.
- **Negative space:** Margins.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Current entry > list.
- **Layering (back to front):** page, list, rules
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Static.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY index templates
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Breaks, intros, outros.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Spread with list on the right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Page.
  - 1080x1080 square: Page.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Page.

## FOL-CMP-C7 Loud collision

- **Focal point:** The lyric, clean, amid colliding secondary type.
- **Lyric placement:** Lyric in a clear zone; secondary type (title, artist, section words) overlapping around it.
- **Background structure:** Duotone page.
- **Foreground:** Rotated, overlapping secondary type.
- **Negative space:** Clear zone around the lyric (exclusion).
- **Visual hierarchy:** Lyric > collisions.
- **Layering (back to front):** page, collision type layers, plate fragments, lyric
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat with overlaps.
- **Camera:** Snap reframes.
- **Safe areas:** Lyric clear zone central 70%; nothing overlaps it.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY loud templates, PAIR loud
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Energetic choruses, the loud issue.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Collisions around.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Collisions above and below.
  - 1080x1080 square: Around.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Reduced collisions (half).

## FOL-CMP-C8 Letter

- **Focal point:** The current line in a handwritten-feel serif letter.
- **Lyric placement:** Letter body; salutation is the first line.
- **Background structure:** Laid paper on a desk.
- **Foreground:** Envelope edge, stamp (original).
- **Negative space:** Margins.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Current line > letter > envelope.
- **Layering (back to front):** desk, envelope, letter paper, text
- **Depth and perspective:** Paper on desk.
- **Camera:** Drift down the letter.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY letter templates, PAPR laid
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Addressed lyrics, letters page.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Letter centred, envelope beside.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Letter fills frame.
  - 1080x1080 square: Letter.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Letter.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Accumulative within a page; page turns between sections. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
