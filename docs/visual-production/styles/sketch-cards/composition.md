# Sketch Cards: composition families

Style 9 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


6 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## SKC-CMP-C1 Card on a surface

- **Focal point:** The top card.
- **Lyric placement:** Card at centre, rotated 2-8 degrees.
- **Background structure:** Ground surface.
- **Foreground:** Tools (marker, tape roll) at edges.
- **Negative space:** Ground around the card.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Card big word > pill > thin > doodle > ground.
- **Layering (back to front):** ground, pile, top card, tape, tools
- **Depth and perspective:** Top-down, shallow.
- **Camera:** Locked, slow pull-back.
- **Safe areas:** Card text inside central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CRD, GRD, drawings
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Default.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Card 70% height.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical card 80% height.
  - 1080x1080 square: Card 75%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Card fills width.

## SKC-CMP-C2 Held cue card

- **Focal point:** A card held up by a hand.
- **Lyric placement:** Card centre; hand at the bottom edge.
- **Background structure:** Out-of-focus street or room.
- **Foreground:** Hand silhouette.
- **Negative space:** Background.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Card > hand > background.
- **Layering (back to front):** background plate, hand, card
- **Depth and perspective:** Card near, background blurred.
- **Camera:** Handheld sway.
- **Safe areas:** Card text central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** HND hands, CRD poster
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Addressed lyrics, hooks.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Card 65% height.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Card 75% height.
  - 1080x1080 square: 70%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Card fills width.

## SKC-CMP-C3 The pile

- **Focal point:** The newest card on a growing pile.
- **Lyric placement:** Top card, offset from the previous.
- **Background structure:** Ground.
- **Foreground:** Pile edges.
- **Negative space:** Shrinks as the pile grows.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Top card > pile.
- **Layering (back to front):** ground, pile (baked), top card
- **Depth and perspective:** Pile thickness.
- **Camera:** Slow pull-back.
- **Safe areas:** Top card inside central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CRD, GRD
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Verses accumulating.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Pile spreads right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Pile spreads down.
  - 1080x1080 square: Diagonal.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Top card only visible fully.

## SKC-CMP-C4 Pinned wall

- **Focal point:** The newest pinned card, highlighted.
- **Lyric placement:** Cards in a loose grid on a wall.
- **Background structure:** Wall surface (cork, plaster, painted wall).
- **Foreground:** Pins and tape.
- **Negative space:** Wall gaps.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Newest card > wall.
- **Layering (back to front):** wall, cards, pins
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat wall.
- **Camera:** Pans to the newest card.
- **Safe areas:** Newest card text central 80% after framing.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CRD, GRD wall
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses (hook cards pinned), list songs.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Grid 4x3.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Grid 2x5.
  - 1080x1080 square: 3x3.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Close framing.

## SKC-CMP-C5 Full-bleed poster

- **Focal point:** The card fills the frame.
- **Lyric placement:** Poster layout.
- **Background structure:** None.
- **Foreground:** Poster elements.
- **Negative space:** Poster's own.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Big word.
- **Layering (back to front):** poster
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** None.
- **Safe areas:** Central 90%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CRD poster, PRT
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses, energetic hooks (today's card look).
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: 4:3 poster with ground slivers.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical poster.
  - 1080x1080 square: Square poster.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Vertical.

## SKC-CMP-C6 Sticky notes cluster

- **Focal point:** The newest note.
- **Lyric placement:** Notes clustered, one per phrase.
- **Background structure:** Wall or desk.
- **Foreground:** Notes overlapping.
- **Negative space:** Surface.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Newest note > cluster.
- **Layering (back to front):** surface, notes
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Notes central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CRD sticky notes
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Rap, dense passages, word-synced.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Cluster spreads.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Column.
  - 1080x1080 square: Cluster.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Column.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Accumulating pile per ground; the pile is the song's memory. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
