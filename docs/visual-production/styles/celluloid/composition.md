# Celluloid: composition families

Style 5 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


7 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## CEL-CMP-C1 Letterbox subtitle

- **Focal point:** The subtitle in the lower bar or lower third of the image area.
- **Lyric placement:** Centred, lower third inside or under the 2.39:1 image area.
- **Background structure:** Film image area: plates, light, stock colour.
- **Foreground:** Letterbox bars.
- **Negative space:** The image area.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Subtitle > image > bars.
- **Layering (back to front):** stock base, image plates (light, leaks), damage, letterbox bars, subtitle, post grain and weave
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Gate weave.
- **Safe areas:** Subtitle box inside the image area's action-safe 90% and above the bottom 14% app zone (subtitle moves into the image area in the app stage).
- **Compatible asset categories:** PLT, STK, TTL subtitle
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Dense lyrics, verses, any song.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: 2.39:1 image with bars.
  - 720x1280 portrait: 4:5 frame centred in 9:16 with bars.
  - 1080x1080 square: 4:5 frame in square with side bars.
  - 390x844 small mobile: As portrait; subtitle 18 px minimum.

## CEL-CMP-C2 Centred title card

- **Focal point:** A centred word or short line.
- **Lyric placement:** Optical centre.
- **Background structure:** Black or stock base with light falloff.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Most of the frame.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Title > grain.
- **Layering (back to front):** base, light, title, damage, grain
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Weave and slow optical push.
- **Safe areas:** Title inside central 70%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** TTL card, REG backlit or typewriter
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Short lines, hooks, openings.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Native.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Native (title wraps to 2-3 lines).
  - 1080x1080 square: Native.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Native.

## CEL-CMP-C3 Scratchboard

- **Focal point:** Scratched letters on black.
- **Lyric placement:** Free within the central 70%.
- **Background structure:** Black leader.
- **Foreground:** Scratched doodle loops around words.
- **Negative space:** Black.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Scratched words > scratched doodles.
- **Layering (back to front):** black leader, scratch doodles, scratched words, dust
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Jitter.
- **Safe areas:** Central 70%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** SCR loops, scratch REG
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Dark, tense, raw songs; Latin only (fallback card elsewhere).
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Native.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Native.
  - 1080x1080 square: Native.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Words larger, fewer doodles.

## CEL-CMP-C4 Projection room

- **Focal point:** The projected rectangle on a wall.
- **Lyric placement:** Lyric inside the projected rectangle (keystoned 3 degrees).
- **Background structure:** Dark room, wall texture.
- **Foreground:** Projector beam with dust, projector silhouette at an edge.
- **Negative space:** Dark room.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Projected text > beam > room.
- **Layering (back to front):** room, wall, projected frame, beam haze, projector silhouette
- **Depth and perspective:** Room depth with the beam.
- **Camera:** Slow drift.
- **Safe areas:** Projected rectangle 60% of the frame; text in its centre 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** PRJ room assets, STK
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Bridges, breaks, nostalgic songs.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Projected rect centre-right, projector left.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Projected rect top, beam from below.
  - 1080x1080 square: Centre.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Rect fills width.

## CEL-CMP-C5 Film strip

- **Focal point:** The frame in the gate window.
- **Lyric placement:** One word per frame on a moving strip; the current frame stops in a window.
- **Background structure:** Light box (diffuse white or warm).
- **Foreground:** Strip with sprocket holes.
- **Negative space:** Light box margins.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Current frame word > strip > light box.
- **Layering (back to front):** light box, strip, frame contents, sprocket holes
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Strip moves vertically (portrait) or horizontally.
- **Safe areas:** Current frame inside central 70%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** GAT strip, TTL frames
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Word-synced rap, list songs; authored timing only.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Horizontal strip.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical strip.
  - 1080x1080 square: Vertical.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Vertical.

## CEL-CMP-C6 Title burst

- **Focal point:** One word at a time, full frame.
- **Lyric placement:** Centred word, scale and face change per word.
- **Background structure:** Stock base changes per cut.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Varies.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Word.
- **Layering (back to front):** base, word, damage
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** None.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** REG burst, TTL burst sets
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses of energetic songs with authored word timing.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Native.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Native.
  - 1080x1080 square: Native.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Native.

## CEL-CMP-C7 Leader and countdown

- **Focal point:** The countdown numeral.
- **Lyric placement:** No lyric (or the first line waiting at the end).
- **Background structure:** Leader stock.
- **Foreground:** Countdown design.
- **Negative space:** Leader.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Numeral > sweep > leader marks.
- **Layering (back to front):** leader base, countdown graphics, damage
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Weave.
- **Safe areas:** Countdown inside central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CNT designs, PLT leader
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Intro, breaks, outro.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Native.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Countdown circle fits width.
  - 1080x1080 square: Native.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Native.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Reel metaphor: damage accumulates within a stock; stocks change at sections like reels. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
