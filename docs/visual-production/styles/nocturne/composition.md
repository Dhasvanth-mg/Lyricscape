# Nocturne: composition families

Style 7 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


6 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## NOC-CMP-C1 Light and text opposite

- **Focal point:** The light pool.
- **Lyric placement:** Text where the light falls, opposite the source.
- **Background structure:** Darkness with room silhouettes at edges.
- **Foreground:** Light source (lamp, window edge).
- **Negative space:** 70-85%.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Text > light > room.
- **Layering (back to front):** darkness, room silhouette, light pool, dust, text, grain
- **Depth and perspective:** Room depth implied by falloff.
- **Camera:** Slow push.
- **Safe areas:** Text inside central 80% and inside the light pool.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LIT, SIL room, WEA dust
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Default.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Light left third, text right two-thirds low.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Light top, text lower third.
  - 1080x1080 square: Light corner, text opposite corner.
  - 390x844 small mobile: As portrait.

## NOC-CMP-C2 Centred halo

- **Focal point:** A single word or short line in a halo.
- **Lyric placement:** Centre.
- **Background structure:** Darkness.
- **Foreground:** Halo.
- **Negative space:** 90%.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Word > halo.
- **Layering (back to front):** darkness, halo, text
- **Depth and perspective:** None.
- **Camera:** None.
- **Safe areas:** Central 60%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LIT halo
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Short lines, hooks in ballads.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Centre.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Centre.
  - 1080x1080 square: Centre.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Centre.

## NOC-CMP-C3 Rolling column

- **Focal point:** The current line in a column.
- **Lyric placement:** Left column (right in RTL), current line at 45% height, completed above, upcoming below (dim).
- **Background structure:** Darkness with a soft vertical light strip behind the column.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Right half.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Current > upcoming > completed.
- **Layering (back to front):** darkness, light strip, column text
- **Depth and perspective:** None.
- **Camera:** None; the column scrolls.
- **Safe areas:** Column inside 8% margins.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LAY column
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Dense lyrics, untimed lyrics, reading mode.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Column 45% wide on the left.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Column full width.
  - 1080x1080 square: Column 60%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Full width.

## NOC-CMP-C4 Window rain

- **Focal point:** Text on the glass plane.
- **Lyric placement:** Text on the glass, upper-middle.
- **Background structure:** Bokeh of city lights beyond the glass.
- **Foreground:** Rain droplets and runnels (masked around text).
- **Negative space:** Dark glass.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Text > bokeh > rain.
- **Layering (back to front):** bokeh plane, window frame silhouette, rain refraction, text, grain
- **Depth and perspective:** Two planes: glass and beyond.
- **Camera:** Focus shifts between planes.
- **Safe areas:** Central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** BOK, WEA rain, SIL window
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Rain and night songs.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Window fills frame.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Window fills frame.
  - 1080x1080 square: Same.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Same.

## NOC-CMP-C5 Low horizon

- **Focal point:** A horizon of distant lights.
- **Lyric placement:** Text low-left above the horizon.
- **Background structure:** Sky gradient and horizon lights; water reflection or street.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Sky.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Text > horizon.
- **Layering (back to front):** sky, horizon lights, reflection, text
- **Depth and perspective:** Far horizon.
- **Camera:** Very slow lateral drift.
- **Safe areas:** Text above the bottom 20%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** BOK horizon, WEA fog
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Sea, travel, longing songs.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Horizon at 70% height.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Horizon at 75%.
  - 1080x1080 square: 70%.
  - 390x844 small mobile: 75%.

## NOC-CMP-C6 Screen glow

- **Focal point:** Text lit by a soft rectangular glow from below.
- **Lyric placement:** Text centre-low above an implied screen glow (no device, no UI).
- **Background structure:** Darkness.
- **Foreground:** Glow from below.
- **Negative space:** Most of the frame.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Text > glow.
- **Layering (back to front):** darkness, glow, text
- **Depth and perspective:** Close.
- **Camera:** None.
- **Safe areas:** Central 70%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** LIT phone glow
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Late-night songs about messages (kept distinct from Screenlife: no interface).
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Glow bottom centre.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Glow bottom.
  - 1080x1080 square: Same.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Same.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

One persistent scene per song half; the light evolves rather than the scene changing. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
