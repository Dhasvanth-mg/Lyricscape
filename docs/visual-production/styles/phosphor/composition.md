# Phosphor: composition families

Style 13 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


5 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## PHO-CMP-C1 Centre writing

- **Focal point:** The writing beam on a centred baseline.
- **Lyric placement:** Baseline at 50% height.
- **Background structure:** Black or graticule.
- **Foreground:** None.
- **Negative space:** Almost everything.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Beam > word.
- **Layering (back to front):** field, graticule, beam, trail, bloom
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** SFN, BCL
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Default.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Baseline width 80%.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Baseline width 90%, larger writing.
  - 1080x1080 square: Centre.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Larger writing, scroll.

- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.

  - 390x844 small mobile: Centre.

## PHO-CMP-C5 Light painting room

- **Focal point:** The warm trail in a dark room.
- **Lyric placement:** Trail mid-frame.
- **Background structure:** Dark room plate (faint).
- **Foreground:** Dust.
- **Negative space:** Darkness.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Trail > room.
- **Layering (back to front):** room plate, trail, dust
- **Depth and perspective:** Shallow room depth.
- **Camera:** Slight drift.
- **Safe areas:** Central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** FUR rooms, BCL warm
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Warm register.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Room wide.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Room tall.
  - 1080x1080 square: Crop.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Crop.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Persistence lengthens across the song (the screen remembers more). Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
