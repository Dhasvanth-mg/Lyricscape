# Panels: composition families

Style 12 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


6 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## PAN-CMP-C1 Grid page

- **Focal point:** The active panel.
- **Lyric placement:** 2-6 panels from the page layout family.
- **Background structure:** Paper gutters.
- **Foreground:** Balloons.
- **Negative space:** Gutters.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Active panel and balloon > other panels.
- **Layering (back to front):** paper, panels (art, tone), borders, balloons, SFX
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat page.
- **Camera:** Panel to panel.
- **Safe areas:** Active balloon inside central 84% after the camera frames it.
- **Compatible asset categories:** PGL, illustrations, BAL
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Verses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Two-page spread, active page centred.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Vertical strip, one panel per screen.
  - 1080x1080 square: Single page.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Camera frames the active panel full.

## PAN-CMP-C2 Splash page

- **Focal point:** One large panel and SFX.
- **Lyric placement:** Full page panel with insets.
- **Background structure:** Art.
- **Foreground:** SFX lettering, balloon.
- **Negative space:** Art's own.
- **Visual hierarchy:** SFX/hook > art.
- **Layering (back to front):** art, tone, SFX, balloon
- **Depth and perspective:** Parallax layers.
- **Camera:** Slow push.
- **Safe areas:** SFX and balloon central 88%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** Illustrations, SFX, SPD
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Choruses, hooks.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide splash.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Tall splash.
  - 1080x1080 square: Square splash.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Splash fills.

## PAN-CMP-C3 Close-up strip

- **Focal point:** A row of close-up panels (hands, eyes, objects).
- **Lyric placement:** Three tall panels side by side.
- **Background structure:** Gutters.
- **Foreground:** Small balloons and captions.
- **Negative space:** Gutters.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Active panel > strip.
- **Layering (back to front):** panels, captions
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Steps along.
- **Safe areas:** Captions central.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CLU illustrations
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Quick lines, rap.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Row of 3-4.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Column of 3.
  - 1080x1080 square: Row of 3.
  - 390x844 small mobile: One at a time.

## PAN-CMP-C4 Wordless establishing

- **Visual hierarchy:** Place.
- **Layering (back to front):** art, tone
- **Depth and perspective:** Atmospheric tone.
- **Camera:** Pan.
- **Safe areas:** Caption central.
- **Compatible asset categories:** PLC illustrations
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Intros, breaks.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Tall crop.
  - 1080x1080 square: Crop.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Crop.

## PAN-CMP-C5 Caption narrative

- **Focal point:** Caption boxes over a single image.
- **Lyric placement:** Captions stepping down the panel.
- **Background structure:** Art.
- **Foreground:** Captions.
- **Negative space:** Art.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Active caption > art.
- **Layering (back to front):** art, captions
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Slow pan down.
- **Safe areas:** Captions central 84%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** BAL captions, illustrations
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Narrative lyrics, quiet verses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Captions left column.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Captions stepped down.
  - 1080x1080 square: Stepped.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Larger captions.

## PAN-CMP-C6 Cover page

- **Focal point:** The song title as a cover.
- **Lyric placement:** Cover layout.
- **Background structure:** Cover art (illustration plus tone).
- **Foreground:** Title lettering.
- **Negative space:** Cover.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Title > art.
- **Layering (back to front):** art, title, cover furniture
- **Depth and perspective:** Flat.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Safe areas:** Title central 88%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** CVR, illustrations
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Intro only.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Cover with paper margins.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Full cover.
  - 1080x1080 square: Cover crop.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Cover crop.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

The page fills panel by panel; each section is a new page. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
