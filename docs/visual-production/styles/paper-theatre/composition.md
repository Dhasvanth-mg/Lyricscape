# Paper Theatre: composition families

Style 3 of 15. Phase 2 planning (generated from planning data).


7 structurally distinct composition families. Each changes the focal type, depth structure or the lyric's relationship to the environment, not only colour. Scene families (scene-families.md) choose among them.

## PTR-CMP-C1 Proscenium stage

- **Focal point:** The stage centre where the light is brightest.
- **Lyric placement:** Banner or card hung on strings in the upper-middle; cut letters in the light band at 40% height.
- **Background structure:** Backlit ground gradient; far layer silhouettes.
- **Foreground:** Proscenium arch and curtains at the edges.
- **Negative space:** Glowing sky region above the set.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Lyric > focal figure > set > arch.
- **Layering (back to front):** ground, far, middle, near set, figures, props, lyric card, proscenium frame
- **Depth and perspective:** 5-7 layers with parallax factors.
- **Camera:** Slow dolly, slight track.
- **Safe areas:** Lyric inside the arch opening's central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** All SIL sets, FIG, FRM arches, TYP cards
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Default for verses and choruses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Wide arch, set spread horizontally.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Tall arch; set stacked (tall trees, towers); card higher.
  - 1080x1080 square: Square arch.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Arch crops to its inner edge; card cap height 5%.

## PTR-CMP-C2 Window stage

- **Focal point:** The scene seen through a window.
- **Lyric placement:** Lyric on a card on the sill below the window, or cut into the window's blind.
- **Background structure:** Interior wall (front-lit dark paper) around the window; the set inside the window.
- **Foreground:** Window frame and sill props.
- **Negative space:** Dark wall.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Lyric card > window scene > wall.
- **Layering (back to front):** window set (ground, far, mid), window frame, wall, sill props, lyric
- **Depth and perspective:** Inside (deep) vs outside (flat wall).
- **Camera:** Static; slow push to the window.
- **Safe areas:** Card inside central 80% x lower 40%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** FRM windows, any set, PRP sill props
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Inward, memory, longing songs; verses.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Window centre-left, card right of sill.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Window upper half, card on sill below.
  - 1080x1080 square: Window centred.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Window fills width.

## PTR-CMP-C3 Pop-up book

- **Focal point:** Pieces rising from the fold.
- **Lyric placement:** Lyric printed on the left page (right page in RTL) or on a tab that pulls.
- **Background structure:** Open book spread with paper texture; set rises from the gutter.
- **Foreground:** Page edges.
- **Negative space:** Page margins.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Lyric on page > pop-up set > book.
- **Layering (back to front):** table, book pages, pop-up pieces in 3 fold planes, page text
- **Depth and perspective:** Pop-up planes at angles (fake 3D by vertical scaling of pieces with fold).
- **Camera:** Top-down three-quarter, slow orbit 4 degrees.
- **Safe areas:** Text inside its page margins.
- **Compatible asset categories:** FRM book spread, SIL sets, TYP printed page
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Narrative songs, story rap, children-friendly.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Spread across the frame.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Single page view (one page visible) with pieces rising toward the viewer.
  - 1080x1080 square: Spread at 90% width.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Single page view.

## PTR-CMP-C4 Lantern

- **Focal point:** A circular disc of light on darkness with silhouettes inside.
- **Lyric placement:** Lyric cut as a ring of text around the disc, or on a card below.
- **Background structure:** Darkness with faint warm spill.
- **Foreground:** Silhouettes inside the disc only.
- **Negative space:** The darkness around the disc (60% of frame).
- **Visual hierarchy:** Lyric > disc scene > darkness.
- **Layering (back to front):** darkness, disc ground, disc set (2-3 layers), ring text or card
- **Depth and perspective:** Shallow, intimate.
- **Camera:** Static; very slow rotation of the disc (lantern turning) 1 degree per second.
- **Safe areas:** Ring text inside the frame with 6% margin; card below disc.
- **Compatible asset categories:** FRM lantern ring, any set (reduced), FIG
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Quiet songs, lullabies, bridges.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Disc 70% of height, card to the right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Disc 70% of width, card below.
  - 1080x1080 square: Disc centred.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Disc fills width; card below.

## PTR-CMP-C5 Panorama scroll

- **Focal point:** The current section of a long landscape passing behind a fixed banner.
- **Lyric placement:** Banner fixed at the top third.
- **Background structure:** Continuous strip of joined sets scrolling sideways (crank panorama).
- **Foreground:** Fixed frame with rollers at the sides.
- **Negative space:** Sky band.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Banner > nearest passing piece > strip.
- **Layering (back to front):** ground, strip far, strip near, rollers frame, banner
- **Depth and perspective:** 2 parallax layers.
- **Camera:** Locked; the strip moves.
- **Safe areas:** Banner inside central 84% width.
- **Compatible asset categories:** SIL sets with seam pieces, TRN sets, FRM rollers
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Journey songs, travel, long verses with steady pace.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Native.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Strip runs vertically (scrolling up), banner on the side? No: banner at top, strip scrolls up beneath it.
  - 1080x1080 square: As landscape at 80% height.
  - 390x844 small mobile: As portrait.

## PTR-CMP-C6 Close-up cut

- **Focal point:** One large silhouette (a hand, a bird, a branch, a moon) with the lyric cut through it.
- **Lyric placement:** Cut letters inside the large piece.
- **Background structure:** Ground glow.
- **Foreground:** The large piece itself (60-80% of the frame).
- **Negative space:** Ground around the piece.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Cut letters > piece shape.
- **Layering (back to front):** ground, large piece with letter holes
- **Depth and perspective:** One layer.
- **Camera:** Slow push.
- **Safe areas:** Letters inside the piece and the central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** PRP large pieces, TYP cut letters
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Hooks, short lines, bridges.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Piece wide.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Piece tall.
  - 1080x1080 square: Centred.
  - 390x844 small mobile: Piece fills width.

## PTR-CMP-C7 Tiny stage in the dark

- **Focal point:** A small lit stage at 30% of the frame.
- **Lyric placement:** Lyric on a card in the darkness beside the stage, front-lit faintly.
- **Background structure:** Darkness.
- **Foreground:** The miniature stage.
- **Negative space:** 70% darkness.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Card > tiny stage.
- **Layering (back to front):** darkness, mini stage (all layers scaled), card
- **Depth and perspective:** Deep: the stage far away.
- **Camera:** Pull-back reveal from C1.
- **Safe areas:** Card central 80%.
- **Compatible asset categories:** Any set at reduced detail, FRM small arch
- **Musical and lyrical conditions:** Bridges, isolation, outros.
- **Responsive:**
  - 1280x720 landscape: Stage left third, card right.
  - 720x1280 portrait: Stage upper half, card below.
  - 1080x1080 square: Stage centre, card below.
  - 390x844 small mobile: As portrait.

## Evolving through a song without chaos

Accumulating stage per section; the set persists across 1-3 sections; pieces arrive and leave on rails. Composition families change only at section boundaries (8-26 s, the director constants) or at a transition listed in motion.md; within a section only the lyric, accumulating elements and the camera move. A composition family is never used for two consecutive sections unless it is a chorus recall.
